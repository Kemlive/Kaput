# Kaput Privacy Pool Liquidity Strategy

## Initial Liquidity Requirements

### Privacy Pool
- Minimum: 100 ETH
- Target: 1,000 ETH
- Goal: 10,000 ETH

### Liquidity Sources
1. **Team Allocation**: 20%
2. **Investors**: 30%
3. **Community**: 50%

## Liquidity Provision

### Phase 1 (Launch)
- Initial: 100 ETH
- Source: Team + early supporters
- Rewards: 10% APY

### Phase 2 (Growth)
- Target: 1,000 ETH
- Source: Community + investors
- Rewards: 15% APY

### Phase 3 (Maturity)
- Goal: 10,000 ETH
- Source: Organic growth
- Rewards: 20% APY

## Incentive Programs

### 1. Liquidity Mining
- Token: KAPUT (future)
- Allocation: 40% of supply
- Duration: 2 years

### 2. Privacy Mining
- Reward: KAPUT tokens
- Action: Use privacy features
- Duration: Ongoing

### 3. Referral Program
- Reward: 5% of fees
- Action: Refer users
- Duration: Ongoing

## Fee Structure
- Privacy Pool Fee: 0.1%
- Bridge Fee: 0.05%
- Sweep Fee: 0.01%
- Total: ~0.16% per transaction
